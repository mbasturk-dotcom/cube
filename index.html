01 — About

Alp Baştürk

Product engineer building fast, accessible interfaces.

  • 8 years shipping product
  • Istanbul, Türkiye

I design and build the front of digital products — the part people actually touch. My work sits between engineering and design: I care about how fast a page paints, how a control feels under the thumb, and whether someone using a screen reader gets the same product everyone else does.

Most of my time goes into design systems, interaction detail, and the unglamorous performance work that makes an interface feel instant. I like problems where the constraint is real — a slow network, a small screen, a deadline.

Outside work I tinker with WebGL, take too many photographs of buildings, and keep a running list of typefaces I will probably never use.
